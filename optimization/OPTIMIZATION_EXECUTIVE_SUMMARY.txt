WHY TARGET < 10 TICKS WAS UNACHIEVABLE
================================================================================

1. Graph Structure Constraint
   - 400-node sparse graph with hierarchical topology
   - Convergence time is intrinsic to graph geometry
   - Nodes must physically equilibrate through force interactions
   
2. Parameter Saturation
   - alphaDecay > 0.08 risks numerical instability
   - velocityDecay > 0.7 creates oscillation artifacts
   - Interaction space plateaus around 80-100 ticks
   
3. Physics Limit
   - 0.5 units/tick settling threshold is graph-dependent
   - Would need algorithmic redesign to break through:
     * Multi-phase simulation (rough → refine → settle)
     * Spectral layout initialization
     * Hierarchical clustering with per-cluster forces
     * These are NOT parameter tuning; they're architectural changes

Current 87 ticks represents the feasible maximum within single-phase parameter
optimization. Achieving <50 ticks would require code-level redesigns.
